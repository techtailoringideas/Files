# SEO growth economics for free-tool sites (Nov 2026)

The network policy blocked primary pages from Ahrefs, Semrush, Seer, Google, Raptive and SEL. Figures come from search summaries unless marked "fetched".

## Key findings

**Google and AI search**
- With an AI Overview present, position-1 CTR was 34.5% lower (Apr 2025, 300k keywords) — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks/). A re-run reportedly found 58% lower (~Feb 2026; secondary, unverified) — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-touts-billions-of-weekly-ai-search-clicks-as-ctr-data-drops-58/).
- Organic CTR on informational queries with AI Overviews fell about 61% from mid-2024. Cited brands got 0.70% CTR versus 0.52% for uncited ones (Sep 2025) — [Seer](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/aio-impact-on-google-ctr-september-2025-update).
- 68.01% of US searches were zero-click in Jan–Apr 2026, versus 60.45% in 2024 (different panels). Open-web clicks fell from 374 to 276 per 1,000 searches. AI Mode took 0.34% of searches — [SparkToro/SEL](https://searchengineland.com/google-zero-click-searches-reach-68-in-early-2026-study-479717).
- Forcing users into AI Mode cut external clicks by 18.8 points (preprint, Mar 2026) — [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/geo/ai-mode-field-experiment-clicks-drop-18-8-points/).
- Action queries are less exposed but not immune:
  - In 2025, transactional keywords triggering an AI Overview rose from 1.98% to 13.94% — [Semrush](https://semrush.com/blog/semrush-ai-overviews-study).
  - Transactional SERPs with an AI Overview then fell 5% (Jul 2026) — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-overviews-commercial-search-study/).
- I found no winner/loser data specific to tool sites. Only 22% of about 400 sites hit by the 2023 helpful content update had gained 20% or more by Aug 2024 — [SER/Gabe](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-helpful-content-update-recovery-data-38358.html).
- Site reputation abuse policy (Nov 2024): third-party content that exploits a host's ranking signals is a violation even with first-party oversight — [SEL](https://searchengineland.com/google-site-reputation-abuse-policy-now-includes-first-party-involvement-or-oversight-of-content-448432).

**CTR curves (sources conflict)**
- Position-1 CTR fell from 28% to 19% between 2024 and 2025 (GrowthSRC) — [Stan Ventures](https://www.stanventures.com/news/ai-overviews-impact-organic-ctrs-32-drop-3635/).
- A 2025 compilation gives 19–27.6% — [Ranktracker](https://www.ranktracker.com/blog/seo-statistics-2025).

**Case studies (self-reported)**
- **Pallyy (2023):** 20+ tools brought about 300k visits/month by targeting keywords where ranking pages had Domain Rating under 20 — [recap](https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/24282908-300k-monthly-visits-from-free-tools-build-with-ai).
- **Signaturely:** one signature tool drew 180k organic visitors and 30k signups in its first year (2021) — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/how-signaturely-acquired-30-000-user-signups-in-12-months-09bcf7bd58).
- **SignHouse:** grew from 0 to 60k organic visitors/month in 6 months (agency case study) — [Embarque](https://www.embarque.io/case-studies/esignature-seo).
- **QuickTools.one:** 11 tools in 4 weeks, about 3k projected users; internal links helped — [dev.to](https://dev.to/eduard_albu/building-boring-tools-that-rank-4-weeks-11-tools-and-a-plot-twist-with-chatgpt-304).
- **Photopea (solo):** about $1M in 2020, mostly from ads — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photopea).
- **Stirling-PDF:** 95.6k GitHub stars and 10.0k forks, open-core with a paid enterprise plan (fetched Nov 3, 2026) — [GitHub](https://github.com/Stirling-Tools/Stirling-PDF).

**Monetization**
- **Raptive:** minimum cut to 25k pageviews (Oct 2025) — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/raptive-drops-pageview-requirement-to-25-000-monthly-visits/). Reportedly at least 50% of traffic must come from tier-1 countries (US, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand; unverified) — [snapshot](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1rw2u5j/snapshots/2026-03-17T20%3A20%3A18.932354Z).
- **Journey by Mediavine:** 1,000 sessions from Jan 2026 — [Productive Blogging](https://www.productiveblogging.com/everything-you-need-to-know-about-journey-by-mediavine/).
- **Ad rates (RPM):** a vendor benchmark (unverified) gives $3–10 per 1,000 pageviews for tool sites — [calculator site](https://superglobalcalculator.com/calculators/digital-marketing/rpm-calculator/).

## Inferences (estimates)
- 40k visits needs about 160k–400k monthly searches where the site ranks in the top 3 (assuming 10–25% CTR).
- At about 60k pageviews and $2–8 RPM, ads would earn $120–480/month.
- Tools the business builds itself are first-party content, so a subfolder carries low site-reputation-abuse risk.

## Gaps
- **Not covered (search budget ran out):** schema/FAQ status, Core Web Vitals, Product Hunt, Hacker News and AI-referral traffic, directories, widgets, subdomains, hosting costs, time-to-rank and keyword difficulty, Ezoic.
- **Unverified prior knowledge:** Google limited FAQ rich results to authoritative government and health sites (Aug 2023).
