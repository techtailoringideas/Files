# SEO growth economics for free-tool sites (notes, Nov 2026)

The network policy blocked these primary pages: Ahrefs, Semrush, Seer, Google Search Central, Raptive and Search Engine Land. Unless a figure is marked "fetched", it comes from search-result summaries. The search budget ran out before several topics were covered (see Gaps).

## Key findings

**Google and AI search**
- When an AI Overview appeared, position-1 CTR was 34.5% lower (Apr 2025, 300k keywords) — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks/). A re-run reportedly found 58% lower (~Feb 2026). This comes from a secondary source and is unverified — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-touts-billions-of-weekly-ai-search-clicks-as-ctr-data-drops-58/).
- On informational queries with AI Overviews, organic CTR fell about 61% from mid-2024. Brands cited in the Overview got 0.70% organic CTR versus 0.52% for uncited brands (Sep 2025) — [Seer](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/aio-impact-on-google-ctr-september-2025-update).
- 68.01% of US Google searches ended without a click in Jan–Apr 2026, versus 60.45% in 2024 (the two years used different panels). Open-web clicks fell from 374 to 276 per 1,000 searches. AI Mode accounted for only 0.34% of searches — [SparkToro via SEL](https://searchengineland.com/google-zero-click-searches-reach-68-in-early-2026-study-479717).
- Forcing users into AI Mode cut external clicks by 18.8 points. This is a preregistered preprint (Mar 2026, n=1,100) — [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/geo/ai-mode-field-experiment-clicks-drop-18-8-points/).
- Action queries are less exposed but not immune:
  - During 2025, the share of transactional keywords that trigger an AI Overview rose from 1.98% to 13.94%. Informational queries fell from 91.3% to 57.1% of all triggers (10M+ keywords) — [Semrush](https://semrush.com/blog/semrush-ai-overviews-study).
  - In the following period, transactional SERPs with an AI Overview fell 5% while commercial ones rose 71% (Jul 2026) — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-overviews-commercial-search-study/).
- I found no winner/loser data specific to tool sites. In the March 2025 core update, forums and aggregators fell (Quora −22.45% in the UK) — [Sistrix](https://www.sistrix.com/blog/google-core-update-march-2025/). Of about 400 sites hit by the Sept 2023 helpful content update, only 22% had gained 20% or more by Aug 2024 — [SER/Glenn Gabe](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-helpful-content-update-recovery-data-38358.html).
- Site reputation abuse policy (Nov 19, 2024): third-party content that exploits a host site's ranking signals is a violation even with first-party oversight. Google enforces it with manual actions — [SEL](https://searchengineland.com/google-site-reputation-abuse-policy-now-includes-first-party-involvement-or-oversight-of-content-448432).

**CTR curves (sources conflict)**
- Position-1 CTR fell from 28% to 19% between 2024 and 2025 (GrowthSRC, 200k keywords) — [Stan Ventures](https://www.stanventures.com/news/ai-overviews-impact-organic-ctrs-32-drop-3635/).
- A 2025 compilation puts position-1 CTR at 19–27.6% — [Ranktracker](https://www.ranktracker.com/blog/seo-statistics-2025).

**Case studies (self-reported or anecdotal)**
- **Pallyy (2023 recap):** 20+ free tools brought in about 300k visits/month. The founder targeted keywords where the ranking pages had Domain Rating (DR) under 20 and a few hundred searches. One commenter said the method failed for them on a new site — [recap](https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/24282908-300k-monthly-visits-from-free-tools-build-with-ai).
- **Signaturely:** one free signature tool drew 180k organic visitors and 30k signups in its first 12 months (from Jan 2021) — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/how-signaturely-acquired-30-000-user-signups-in-12-months-09bcf7bd58). A secondary source later reported about 800k organic visits/month — [BetterLaunch](https://www.betterlaunch.co/playbooks/will-cannon-uplead-3).
- **SignHouse:** grew from 0 to 60k monthly organic visitors in 6 months (agency case study) — [Embarque](https://www.embarque.io/case-studies/esignature-seo).
- **QuickTools.one:** built 11 tools in 4 weeks, with about 3k monthly users projected. Internal links between tools helped — [dev.to](https://dev.to/eduard_albu/building-boring-tools-that-rank-4-weeks-11-tools-and-a-plot-twist-with-chatgpt-304).
- **Photopea (solo developer):** earned about $1M in 2020, mostly from ads — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photopea). A Reddit AMA is credited for growth to 12M users — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/how-reddit-ama-earned-photopea-12m-monthly-users-7c66c1d9ad).
- **Stirling-PDF:** had about 48k GitHub stars in early 2025 — [ecosyste.ms](https://opencollective.ecosyste.ms/projects/216240). On Nov 3, 2026 it had 95.6k stars and 10.0k forks, with an open-core paid Server/Enterprise plan (fetched) — [GitHub](https://github.com/Stirling-Tools/Stirling-PDF).

**Monetization**
- **Raptive** cut its minimum to 25k monthly pageviews on Oct 16, 2025 — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/raptive-drops-pageview-requirement-to-25-000-monthly-visits/). Below 100k, it reportedly requires at least 50% of traffic from the US, UK, Canada, Australia or New Zealand (unverified) — [snapshot](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1rw2u5j/snapshots/2026-03-17T20%3A20%3A18.932354Z). Original content is required — [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/raptive-drops-traffic-requirement-by-75-to-25000-views/558780/).
- **Journey by Mediavine** has required 1,000 sessions since Jan 15, 2026, down from 10k — [Productive Blogging](https://www.productiveblogging.com/everything-you-need-to-know-about-journey-by-mediavine/). The full Mediavine program is reportedly gated at $5k in annual ad revenue, which conflicts with the older 50k-session figure — [Jupiter](https://www.jupiter.co/blog/mediavine-requirements-2026-how-to-qualify).
- **Ad rates (RPM):** a vendor benchmark (unverified) puts tool sites at $3–10 per 1,000 pageviews — [calculator site](https://superglobalcalculator.com/calculators/digital-marketing/rpm-calculator/). The same site earns about 3x more from US traffic than from Asian traffic — [Renlar](https://renlar.com/tools/adsense-earnings-calculator).

## Inferences (estimates)
- 40k visits/month at a blended 10–25% CTR needs about 160k–400k monthly searches across keywords where the site ranks in the top 3.
- 40k visits is roughly 60k pageviews. At $2–8 RPM that is about $120–480/month from ads. Tool traffic from countries outside Raptive's list may fail its geography rule.
- Tools that the business builds itself are first-party content, so a subfolder on the existing site carries little site-reputation-abuse risk.
