# Business-document, text, marketing/SEO, developer and calculator tool niches (Nov 2026)

Stopped early on budget. WebFetch could not reach ahrefs.com or rankhero.com, and the search limit was reached. All volumes and traffic figures are third-party estimates.

## Key findings
- **QR:** "qr code generator" gets about 538K US searches a month (Ahrefs snapshot, Feb 2025) ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/websites/the-qrcode-generator.com)). "free qr code generator" gets 87K and "qr code generator free" 63K ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/websites/qr-code-generator.com)). The main complaint is that "free" dynamic codes stop working when the trial ends. One reviewer paid $220 and the code was still disabled ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/177261/QR-Code-Generator-Pro/reviews/)). Others report surprise annual charges ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.qr-code-generator.com?page=8)). GetQR's help page says dynamic codes stop working after the trial ([GetQR](https://api.getqr.com/help/qr-code-not-working)). The 2026 press pieces on this promote FreeQR ([example](https://centralbucksnews.com/news/2026/apr/27/free-qr-code-generator-the-most-misleading-search-result-on-the-internet/)).
- **Invoice:** "invoice generator" gets about 183K searches a month at KD 63. "invoice template" gets about 111K at KD 43 (Ahrefs blog, about Aug 2026) ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/the-free-tools-seo-strategy/)).
- **Resume:** "resume builder" gets about 225–242K searches a month ([Indexator](https://indexator.ai/companies/kickresume), [Toolbit](https://www.toolbit.ai/ai-tool/resume-builder)). Users complain that the paywall appears only at the download step (Reddit mirror, Dec 2025) ([post](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1pvittt/snapshots/2025-12-26T08%3A00%3A21.210554Z)). A competitor's antitrust suit alleges that Bold charges 10–20x the initial fee every four weeks. This is an unproven allegation ([Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/antitrust/bold-accused-in-lawsuit-of-monopolizing-online-resume-building), [Rocket Resume](https://rocket-resume.com/rocket-resume-v-bold-antitrust-lawsuit)).
- **Word counter:** wordcounter.net got about 2M search visits in Oct 2026. About 822K of them came from "word counter", which has about 792K US searches a month ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/websites/wordcounter.net)).
- **AI humanizer:** Volume estimates conflict. Ahrefs shows 47K (Apr 2025) ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/websites/humanizeai.io)), while other tools show about 1.1–1.45M ([Toolbit](https://www.toolbit.ai/ai-tool/humanize-ai-5155), [Indexator](https://indexator.ai/companies/humanize-ai-content-detector)).
- **Calculators:** calculator.net got about 27M search visits in Jun 2025 ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/top/calculator.net)). Ahrefs' guide says big brands dominate head terms such as "mortgage calculator" and recommends targeting the long tail ([Optimixed summary](https://www.optimixed.com/the-free-tools-seo-strategy-how-to-rank-with-calculators-converters-and-generators/)).
- **JSON formatters:** In Nov 2025, watchTowr scraped more than 80,000 saved pastes from JSONFormatter and CodeBeautify. They included Active Directory credentials and cloud keys, and someone tried a planted AWS canary key about 48 hours later ([Help Net Security](https://helpnetsecurity.com/2025/11/25/code-formatting-sites-exposing-secrets), [Security Affairs](https://securityaffairs.com/185150/security/thousands-of-sensitive-secrets-published-on-jsonformatter-and-codebeautify.html)).

## Risk of AI answers and policy problems
- AI Overviews appear on 36% of informational queries but only 5% of transactional ones (Seer data, reported secondhand) ([summary](https://ai-visibility-37g.pages.dev/blog/what-triggers-ai-overview)). A randomized study, also reported secondhand, found a 39.8% drop in clicks and no measurable change on transactional queries ([summary](https://ecorpit.com/ai-overviews-randomized-study-click-loss-content-strategy-2026/)). In 2026, fewer than a third of Google searches led to a click ([SparkToro](https://sparktoro.com/blog/in-2026-less-than-one-third-of-google-searches-still-send-a-click/)). Searches like "time in Tokyo" now end without a click (agency opinion) ([source](https://www.digitalthriveai.com/en-ca/resources/ai-and-automation/google-showing-zero-results-again-for-many-time-calculations-conversions-search-results/)).
- The FTC finalized an order against Workado because its claim of 98% AI-detection accuracy was false (reported 31 Oct 2025) ([report](https://www.14news.com/2025/10/31/ftc-cracking-down-ai-detection-tools)). A University of Maryland study found that detectors wrongly flagged human-written text about 6.8% of the time on average ([Boston Globe, May 2025](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2025/05/18/lifestyle/new-headache-honest-students-proving-they-didnt-use-ai)).
- Google Ads bans tools that enable academic dishonesty, such as essay generators ([Google](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6016086?hl=en)). Google's publisher policies ban content that helps users mislead others ([Google](https://support.google.com/publisherpolicies/answer/10502938?hl=en)). Humanizers and fake receipt or document generators are therefore a risk for ad-funded sites.

## Engineering-as-marketing and indie evidence
- Secondary write-ups (exact page not confirmed) say HubSpot's Website Grader has graded more than 4M sites since 2006 and was built by 2 engineers in 2 weeks. CoSchedule says users have analyzed more than 4M headlines ([CoSchedule](https://coschedule.com/blog/what-makes-headline-studio-the-best-tool-since-headline-analyzer)). I found no lead or conversion figures for either.
- The indie evidence is weak. One solo tools site got 41% of its traffic from Reddit and 2% from Google (Mar 2026) ([Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/product/myclaw-tools-2)). Another got 71% of its first-month traffic from ChatGPT referrals ([dev.to](https://dev.to/eduard_albu/building-boring-tools-that-rank-4-weeks-11-tools-and-a-plot-twist-with-chatgpt-304)). I found no verified indie site getting 10k+ monthly Google visits in these niches in 2025.

## Ranked shortlist (my judgment)
1. A JSON/CSV/YAML formatter-validator that runs entirely in the browser. It fills the trust gap the watchTowr leak exposed, costs almost nothing to host and appeals to tech clients.
2. A static QR generator whose codes never expire, plus an honest dynamic option. Target the long tail: no sign-up, no expiration, Wi-Fi and vCard codes.
3. An invoice, quote and estimate generator with no sign-up and no watermark.
4. An HTML email-signature generator. Users want a file they can paste into their email client, which AI answers don't replace.
5. SEO utilities such as a UTM builder, SERP preview and schema generator. They are for winning clients, not for traffic.

Avoid: AI detectors and humanizers, downloaders, mortgage and other high-stakes finance calculators, converters that Google answers with its own widget, and backlink checkers.

## Gaps
- I found no verified volume or KD for: barcode, quote/estimate, cover letter, signature, case converter, citation, regex, password, UTM, schema, robots.txt, GPA/age/percentage and salary tools. US/global splits and YouTube-tool terms of service were also not checked.
