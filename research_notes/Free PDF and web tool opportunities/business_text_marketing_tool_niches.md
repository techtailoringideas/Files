# Business-document, text, marketing/SEO, developer and calculator tool niches (Nov 2026)

Stopped early on budget. ahrefs.com and rankhero.com could not be fetched. All figures are third-party estimates.

## Key findings
- **QR:** "qr code generator" gets about 538K US searches a month (Feb 2025) ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/websites/the-qrcode-generator.com)). "free qr code generator" gets 87K and "qr code generator free" 63K ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/websites/qr-code-generator.com)). Codes die when the trial ends, even after paying $220 ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/177261/QR-Code-Generator-Pro/reviews/)). Users report surprise annual charges ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.qr-code-generator.com?page=8)). Trial codes stop working ([GetQR](https://api.getqr.com/help/qr-code-not-working)). The 2026 press pieces on this promote FreeQR ([example](https://centralbucksnews.com/news/2026/apr/27/free-qr-code-generator-the-most-misleading-search-result-on-the-internet/)).
- **Invoice:** "invoice generator" gets about 183K searches a month at KD 63. "invoice template" gets about 111K at KD 43 (about Aug 2026) ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/the-free-tools-seo-strategy/)).
- **Resume:** "resume builder" gets about 225–242K searches a month ([Indexator](https://indexator.ai/companies/kickresume), [Toolbit](https://www.toolbit.ai/ai-tool/resume-builder)). Users complain about paywalls at the download step (Dec 2025) ([Reddit mirror](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1pvittt/snapshots/2025-12-26T08%3A00%3A21.210554Z)). A competitor's antitrust suit alleges Bold charges 10–20x the initial fee every four weeks (unproven) ([Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/antitrust/bold-accused-in-lawsuit-of-monopolizing-online-resume-building)).
- **Word counter:** wordcounter.net got about 2M search visits in Oct 2026. About 822K came from "word counter", which has about 792K US searches ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/websites/wordcounter.net)).
- **AI humanizer:** Estimates conflict: 47K (Apr 2025) ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/websites/humanizeai.io)) against 1.1–1.45M ([Toolbit](https://www.toolbit.ai/ai-tool/humanize-ai-5155), [Indexator](https://indexator.ai/companies/humanize-ai-content-detector)).
- **Calculators:** calculator.net got about 27M search visits in Jun 2025 ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/top/calculator.net)). Big brands dominate head terms, so the long tail is the target ([Optimixed](https://www.optimixed.com/the-free-tools-seo-strategy-how-to-rank-with-calculators-converters-and-generators/)).
- **JSON formatters:** In Nov 2025, watchTowr scraped more than 80,000 saved pastes from JSONFormatter and CodeBeautify, including credentials and cloud keys. Someone tried a planted AWS key about 48 hours later ([Help Net Security](https://helpnetsecurity.com/2025/11/25/code-formatting-sites-exposing-secrets)).

## Risk of AI answers and policy problems
- AI Overviews appear on 36% of informational and 5% of transactional queries (Seer, secondhand) ([summary](https://ai-visibility-37g.pages.dev/blog/what-triggers-ai-overview)). Fewer than a third of Google searches led to a click in 2026 ([SparkToro](https://sparktoro.com/blog/in-2026-less-than-one-third-of-google-searches-still-send-a-click/)). Searches like "time in Tokyo" end without a click ([agency opinion](https://www.digitalthriveai.com/en-ca/resources/ai-and-automation/google-showing-zero-results-again-for-many-time-calculations-conversions-search-results/)).
- The FTC finalized an order against Workado over its false claim of 98% detection accuracy (Oct 2025) ([report](https://www.14news.com/2025/10/31/ftc-cracking-down-ai-detection-tools)). Detectors wrongly flagged human text about 6.8% of the time ([Boston Globe, May 2025](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2025/05/18/lifestyle/new-headache-honest-students-proving-they-didnt-use-ai)).
- Google Ads bans tools that enable academic dishonesty ([Google](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6016086?hl=en)), and its publisher policies ban content that helps users mislead others ([Google](https://support.google.com/publisherpolicies/answer/10502938?hl=en)). Humanizers and fake-document generators are risky for ad-funded sites.

## Engineering-as-marketing and indie evidence
- Secondary write-ups (page not confirmed) say HubSpot's Website Grader graded more than 4M sites since 2006. CoSchedule says users analyzed more than 4M headlines ([CoSchedule](https://coschedule.com/blog/what-makes-headline-studio-the-best-tool-since-headline-analyzer)). I found no lead figures for either.
- Indie evidence is weak. One tools site got 41% of its traffic from Reddit and 2% from Google (Mar 2026) ([Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/product/myclaw-tools-2)). Another got 71% from ChatGPT referrals ([dev.to](https://dev.to/eduard_albu/building-boring-tools-that-rank-4-weeks-11-tools-and-a-plot-twist-with-chatgpt-304)). I found no verified indie site with 10k+ monthly Google visits.

## Ranked shortlist (my judgment)
1. A JSON/CSV formatter that runs entirely in the browser. It fills the trust gap from the leak and appeals to tech clients.
2. A QR generator whose static codes never expire, aimed at long-tail searches ("no sign up", Wi-Fi, vCard).
3. An invoice and quote generator with no sign-up and no watermark.
4. An HTML email-signature generator, which produces something AI answers can't replace.
5. SEO utilities (UTM builder, SERP preview, schema generator), for winning clients rather than traffic.

Avoid: detectors and humanizers, downloaders, mortgage calculators, converters Google answers itself, and backlink checkers.

## Gaps
- I found no verified volume or KD for barcode, quote, cover letter, case converter, citation, regex, password, UTM, schema, GPA and salary tools. US/global splits were not checked.
