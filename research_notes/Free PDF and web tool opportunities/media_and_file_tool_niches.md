# Media and File Tool Niches (non-PDF): Demand, Competition, Gaps

Incomplete: research stopped early on budget. This session's network blocks ahrefs.com, semrush.com and reddit.com, and the search budget ran out. Figures come from search snippets of public Semrush and Exploding Topics (ET) pages; all are third-party estimates.

## What are demand, difficulty and incumbent traffic?

### Takeaway
Demand is large and sits mostly with a few incumbents that get heavy direct traffic. The exact-target long tail is crowded, but mostly with small sites.

### Cited Findings
- Keyword volumes, Aug 2026 (probably US): "video converter" 1M, KD 69. "heic to jpg" about 246K, where FreeConvert ranks #3. "mp4 to mp3" 135K, KD 46. "video to mp3" 74K, KD 72. "file compressor" 90.5K, KD 95. [ET](https://analytics.explodingtopics.com/website/freeconvert.com)
- "heic to jpg": 246,000 a month, CPC $0.47, 49% of heictojpg.com's traffic. The site gets about 1.9M visits a month (page dated Jun 2025). [Semrush](https://sv.semrush.com/website/heictojpg.com/overview)
- "compress jpeg" 12.1K (US), "jpeg compression" 8.1K, "image compressor jpg" 5.4K. [seodata.dev](https://www.seodata.dev/keyword/compress-jpeg)
- "background remover" 3.8–4.3M a month and "remove bg" 3.8M (undated, probably global). [Toolbit](https://www.toolbit.ai/ai-tool/remove-bg-15307); [Indexator](https://indexator.ai/companies/removal-ai-ai-background-remover)
- "image upscaler": ET gives 368K (+163%, Nov 2024). [ET](https://explodingtopics.com/topic/image-upscaler-KdL22fcu) Indexator gives 527K. [Indexator](https://indexator.ai/companies/image-upscaler) The two sources conflict.
- Monthly visits:
  - iLoveIMG: 34.4M (Nov 2025), about half from Google. [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/website/iloveimg.com/overview/)
  - TinyPNG: 4.3M (Dec 2025), 73% direct. [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/website/tinypng.com/overview/)
  - ezgif: 12.0M (Dec 2025). [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/website/ezgif.com/overview/)
  - FreeConvert: 52.6M (Aug 2026), 26.9M of it organic. [ET](https://analytics.explodingtopics.com/website/freeconvert.com)
  - CloudConvert: 31.5M (Jan 2026). [ET](https://analytics.explodingtopics.com/website/cloudconvert.com)
  - vocalremover.org: 12.7M (Nov 2025), with about 125K visits referred by ChatGPT. [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/website/vocalremover.org/overview/)
- Mid-tier sites fell in 2025–26:
  - imageresizer.com: 8.6M (Sep 2025) down to 5.4M (Apr 2026). [Semrush](https://sv.semrush.com/website/imageresizer.com/overview); [ET](https://analytics.explodingtopics.com/website/imageresizer.com)
  - 11zon: 10.2M (Feb 2025) down to 5.6M (Apr 2025). [Semrush](https://cw.semrush.com/website/11zon.com/overview)
  - remove.bg: organic traffic down 13.96% month on month (Sep 2025). [Semrush](https://cw.semrush.com/website/remove.bg/overview)
- Passport photos: idphoto4you about 406K visits (Dec 2025). [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/website/makepassportphoto.com/competitors) persofoto about 21K (Jul 2025). [Semrush](https://sv.semrush.com/website/persofoto.com/overview)
- Small sites (smallseotools, pi7, resizekb.tech, a GitHub Pages tool) crowd the "compress image to 20kb" results. [Example](https://orbitwebtools.github.io/image-resizer-20kb/)

### Inferences
- "mp4 to mp3" (KD 46) and "heic to jpg" (246K) have the best volume-to-difficulty ratio in this data.
- Mid-tier rankings are shifting (cause unverified).

### Gaps
- No volume/KD found for: video compression to a size or platform, trimmer, audio cutter, screen recorder, captions, OCR, collage, watermark, blur, EXIF, SVG, favicon, GIF maker, ZIP/RAR, WebP/AVIF, passport photos by country. US/global splits were rarely available.

## What are the incumbents' limits and user complaints, and what can run in the browser?

### Takeaway
Barely researched.

### Cited Findings

## Which opportunities rank best? (provisional)

### Takeaway
Low confidence: based on the data above plus the unverified assumption that each tool runs in the browser.

### Inferences
1. HEIC to JPG, batch and with no upload (246K volume, one dedicated incumbent).
2. MP4 to MP3 / video to audio (135K, KD 46).
3. Image compressor/resizer to an exact KB or pixel target (crowded only with small sites).
4. Video compressor to an exact size for Discord, WhatsApp or email (8mb.video's caps leave a gap).
5. Passport and ID photos by country (idphoto4you's roughly 406K visits suggest demand).
