# Online PDF Tools: Market, Demand and Gaps (notes, Nov 2026)

How these notes were gathered: the shared search budget ran out partway through, and the Semrush, Similarweb and dev.to pages could not be opened. The figures below therefore come from search-result snippets. Every traffic and keyword figure is a third-party estimate, and the sources often disagree.

## Key findings

### Traffic (monthly visits, estimated)
- iLovePDF: 274.7M on a Similarweb page dated Nov 2025 ([Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/website/smallpdf.com/)), and 325.1M in Sep 2026 ([Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/website/ilovepdf.com/overview/)).
- Smallpdf: 42.8M in Aug 2026 ([Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/website/smallpdf.com/overview/)). On 14 Jul 2026 it sold its subsidiary PDF Tools AG to Apryse. Smallpdf itself was not sold ([Apryse](https://apryse.com/blog/apryse-acquires-Pdftools)).
- Sejda: 20.6M in May 2026 ([Exploding Topics](https://analytics.explodingtopics.com/website/sejda.com)) against 37.7M in Sep 2026 ([Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/website/sejda.com/overview/)). About 60% of its organic search traffic comes from India ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/websites/sejda.com)).
- PDF24: about 17.3M in Jul 2026 ([Exploding Topics](https://analytics.explodingtopics.com/website/pdf24.org)), with a Domain Rating of 77 ([AhrefsTop](https://ahrefstop.com/websites/pdf24.org)).
- PDF Guru: 12.1M in Sep 2026 ([Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/website/pdfguru.com/overview/)), mostly from paid search ([Toolify](https://www.toolify.ai/tool/ai-pdf-summarizer-by-pdf-guru)). pdfFiller: about 10–12M ([Hypestat](https://hypestat.com/info/pdffiller.com)).
- PDFgear: about 3.4M in Jun 2026 ([SeekTool](https://seektool.ai/ai/pdfgear-com/alternatives)), up from about 860K per quarter in 2023 ([Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/website/pdfgear.com/vs/zonepdf.com/)).
- TinyWow: about 2M and falling, with 392K organic search visits in Oct 2026 ([AhrefsTop](https://ahrefstop.com/websites/tinywow.com)).

### Search demand and keyword difficulty (KD)
- "pdf to word": about 4.0M searches, country not stated ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/websites/pdf2doc.com)). An Ahrefs example gives 220K searches with a KD of 81 ([Ahrefs blog](https://ahrefs.com/blog/the-free-tools-seo-strategy/)).
- "pdf editor": 1.5M+. "online pdf editor": 731K. Both figures come from a profile weighted toward India ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/websites/sejda.com)).
- "pdf to jpg": about 190K. "jpg to pdf": about 168K ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/websites/smallpdf.com)).
- "unlock pdf": 33.1K, KD 38. "remove password from pdf": 18.1K, KD 22. The source does not name its keyword tool ([DEV](https://dev.to/eduard_albu/week-6-golden-keywords-honest-limitations-and-why-im-building-a-directory-hit-list-4g3g)).
- "pdf bank statement to csv": 90 searches, KD 5, traffic potential 3,300 ([Ahrefs blog](https://ahrefs.com/blog/the-free-tools-seo-strategy/)).

### Who ranks, and where the openings are
- Large incumbents hold the head terms.
- For "compress pdf to 100kb/200kb", small Indian sites (toolbench.in, examresize.online, myexamphoto.in) show up next to Adobe ([Toolbench](https://www.toolbench.in/tools/compress-pdf)).
- Bank statement queries return small vendors ([DocuClipper](https://www.docuclipper.com/solutions/bank-statement-to-excel/), [StatementSheet](https://statementsheet.com/)).
- Both checks come from my search tool, not from Google results pages.

### User complaints
- Free-tier limits:
  - Smallpdf allows 2 tasks a day ([ExactPDF](https://exactpdf.com/blog/smallpdf-free-limits-2026)).
  - Sejda allows 3 tasks an hour, up to 50MB or 200 pages ([WPS](https://www.wps.com/blog/a-comprehensive-guide-to-sejda-pdf-editor-2026-updated-post/)). Both of these sources are competitors.
  - Adobe allows one free transaction without signing in, and premium tools once every 30 days ([Adobe](https://helpx.adobe.com/document-cloud/faq/try-acrobat-online-services.html)).
  - CloudConvert allows 10 credits a day ([CloudConvert](https://cloudconvert.com/pricing)).
- Billing traps:
  - PDF Guru's Trustpilot page (36.5K reviews) notes unexpected subscriptions after a small one-document fee. One review is titled "$0.48 Trial Turned Into a $64.35 Subscription Charge" ([Trustpilot](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/pdfguru.com)).
  - A Smallpdf reviewer wrote "cancelled… still they withdrew $144" ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/smallpdf.com)).
- Privacy: in Mar 2025 the FBI warned that fake converter sites deliver malware ([BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/fbi-warnings-are-true-fake-file-converters-do-push-malware/)).
- Redaction: the Dec 2025 Epstein release had black boxes drawn over live text, and copy-paste revealed what was underneath ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/daveywinder/2025/12/26/epstein-files-hacked---all-you-need-to-know/)).

### New entrants
- Stirling-PDF: about 95.6K GitHub stars, up from 63K in Jul 2025. It claims 30M+ downloads ([GitHub](https://github.com/Stirling-Tools/Stirling-PDF), [X](https://x.com/StirlingPDF), [Stirling](https://www.stirling.com/)).
- BentoPDF: about 16K stars. It took off after its creator posted it on r/selfhosted ([GitHub](https://github.com/alam00000/bentopdf)).
- Bank Statement Converter, run by one person: revenue grew from $3.2K in 2021 to $318K in 2024, as reported by the founder ([X](https://x.com/BallerIndustry/status/1902529043653951745)).
- Show HN now has many in-browser "no upload" PDF tools ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48729230)).

## Ranked opportunities (my inference)
1. **Compress to an exact KB size for exam and government uploads.** Small sites already rank, and it is cheap to build in the browser. Demand is mostly from India, so ad revenue per visit is low.
2. **Bank statement PDF to Excel or CSV.** It has proven revenue and a long tail of searches for individual banks. Getting tables right is hard. Keeping files on the user's device is the selling point.
3. **Redaction that truly removes text, plus a tool that checks whether a redaction worked.** This is a new trust gap.
4. **Core tools with no uploads and no limits.** Use this as positioning rather than as a niche, because the space is crowded.
5. **PDF accessibility checker.** The ADA Title II deadlines moved to Apr 2027 and Apr 2028 ([Duane Morris](https://www.duanemorris.com/alerts/doj_extends_ada_title_ii_digital_accessibility_deadlines_one_year_0426.html)). Buyers are mostly organisations, and search volume is lower.

Avoid the head terms, where KD is around 80.

## Gaps
- No search volumes or KD for merge, compress, sign, split, rotate, OCR, fill, redact, flatten, watermark, page numbers, scanned-PDF editing or most long-tail queries.
- No current traffic figures for DocHub, Canva, Adobe's online tools, Xodo or PDFescape.
- No checks of actual Google results pages, and few complaint counts from Reddit, G2 or HN.
