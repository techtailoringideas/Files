# Online PDF Tools: Market, Demand and Gaps (Nov 2026)

Method: search budget ran out early; Semrush and Similarweb pages would not open, so figures come from search snippets. All are third-party estimates that often disagree.

## Key findings

### Traffic (monthly visits, estimated)
- iLovePDF: 274.7M, Nov 2025 ([Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/website/smallpdf.com/)); 325.1M in Sep 2026 ([Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/website/ilovepdf.com/overview/)).
- Smallpdf: 42.8M in Aug 2026 ([Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/website/smallpdf.com/overview/)). It sold subsidiary PDF Tools AG (not Smallpdf itself) to Apryse, 14 Jul 2026 ([Apryse](https://apryse.com/blog/apryse-acquires-Pdftools)).
- Sejda: 20.6M in May 2026 ([Exploding Topics](https://analytics.explodingtopics.com/website/sejda.com)) against 37.7M in Sep 2026 ([Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/website/sejda.com/overview/)). 60% of organic traffic is Indian ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/websites/sejda.com)).
- PDF24: about 17.3M in Jul 2026 ([Exploding Topics](https://analytics.explodingtopics.com/website/pdf24.org)); Domain Rating 77 ([AhrefsTop](https://ahrefstop.com/websites/pdf24.org)).
- PDF Guru: 12.1M in Sep 2026 ([Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/website/pdfguru.com/overview/)), mostly from paid search ([Toolify](https://www.toolify.ai/tool/ai-pdf-summarizer-by-pdf-guru)). pdfFiller: about 10–12M ([Hypestat](https://hypestat.com/info/pdffiller.com)).
- PDFgear: ~3.4M, Jun 2026 ([SeekTool](https://seektool.ai/ai/pdfgear-com/alternatives)), up from about 860K per quarter in 2023 ([Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/website/pdfgear.com/vs/zonepdf.com/)).
- TinyWow: ~2M, falling; 392K organic visits, Oct 2026 ([AhrefsTop](https://ahrefstop.com/websites/tinywow.com)).

### Search demand and keyword difficulty (KD)
- "pdf to word": about 4.0M, country not stated ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/websites/pdf2doc.com)). An Ahrefs example gives 220K with KD 81 ([Ahrefs blog](https://ahrefs.com/blog/the-free-tools-seo-strategy/)).
- "pdf editor": 1.5M+. "online pdf editor": 731K (India-weighted) ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/websites/sejda.com)).
- "pdf to jpg": about 190K. "jpg to pdf": about 168K ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/websites/smallpdf.com)).
- "unlock pdf": 33.1K, KD 38. "remove password from pdf": 18.1K, KD 22 (tool unnamed) ([DEV](https://dev.to/eduard_albu/week-6-golden-keywords-honest-limitations-and-why-im-building-a-directory-hit-list-4g3g)).
- "pdf bank statement to csv": 90 searches, KD 5, traffic potential 3,300 ([Ahrefs blog](https://ahrefs.com/blog/the-free-tools-seo-strategy/)).

### Who ranks
- Small Indian sites (toolbench.in, examresize.online, myexamphoto.in) appear next to Adobe for "compress pdf to 100kb/200kb" ([Toolbench](https://www.toolbench.in/tools/compress-pdf)).
- Bank-statement queries return small vendors ([DocuClipper](https://www.docuclipper.com/solutions/bank-statement-to-excel/), [StatementSheet](https://statementsheet.com/)).
- Checked with a search tool, not Google.

### User complaints
- Free-tier limits:
  - Smallpdf: 2 tasks a day ([ExactPDF](https://exactpdf.com/blog/smallpdf-free-limits-2026)).
  - Sejda: 3 tasks an hour, up to 50MB or 200 pages ([WPS](https://www.wps.com/blog/a-comprehensive-guide-to-sejda-pdf-editor-2026-updated-post/)). Both are competitor sources.
  - Adobe: one transaction without sign-in; premium tools once per 30 days ([Adobe](https://helpx.adobe.com/document-cloud/faq/try-acrobat-online-services.html)).
  - CloudConvert: 10 credits a day ([CloudConvert](https://cloudconvert.com/pricing)).
- Billing:
  - PDF Guru (36.5K Trustpilot reviews): one review is titled "$0.48 Trial Turned Into a $64.35 Subscription Charge" ([Trustpilot](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/pdfguru.com)).
  - A Smallpdf reviewer: "cancelled… still they withdrew $144" ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/smallpdf.com)).
- Privacy: in Mar 2025 the FBI warned that fake converter sites deliver malware ([BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/fbi-warnings-are-true-fake-file-converters-do-push-malware/)).
- Redaction: in the Dec 2025 Epstein files, black boxes sat over live text that copy-paste revealed ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/daveywinder/2025/12/26/epstein-files-hacked---all-you-need-to-know/)).

### New entrants
- Stirling-PDF: ~95.6K GitHub stars (63K in Jul 2025); claims 30M+ downloads ([GitHub](https://github.com/Stirling-Tools/Stirling-PDF), [X](https://x.com/StirlingPDF), [Stirling](https://www.stirling.com/)).
- BentoPDF: about 16K stars; took off on r/selfhosted ([GitHub](https://github.com/alam00000/bentopdf)).
- Bank Statement Converter (one founder): revenue $3.2K (2021) to $318K (2024), founder-reported ([X](https://x.com/BallerIndustry/status/1902529043653951745)).
- In-browser "no upload" PDF tools are now common on Show HN ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48729230)).

## Ranked opportunities (my inference)
1. **Compress to an exact KB size for exam and government uploads.** Small sites rank; cheap in-browser build. Mostly Indian demand, so low ad revenue.
2. **Bank statement PDF to Excel/CSV.** Proven revenue and a long tail of bank-specific searches. Tables are hard; on-device processing sells.
3. **Redaction that really removes text, plus a redaction checker.** A new trust gap.
4. **No-upload, no-limit core tools.** Positioning, not a niche; crowded.
5. **PDF accessibility checker.** ADA Title II deadlines moved to Apr 2027 and Apr 2028 ([Duane Morris](https://www.duanemorris.com/alerts/doj_extends_ada_title_ii_digital_accessibility_deadlines_one_year_0426.html)). Organisational buyers, lower volume.

Avoid head terms, held by large incumbents (KD about 80).

## Gaps
- No volume or KD for merge, compress, sign, split, rotate, OCR, fill, redact, flatten, watermark, page numbers, scanned-PDF editing or most long tails.
- No current traffic for DocHub, Canva, Adobe online, Xodo or PDFescape.
- No Google results-page checks; few Reddit, G2 or HN complaint counts.
